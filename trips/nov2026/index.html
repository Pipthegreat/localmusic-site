Leg 01 · Santiago de Chile

The Andean spring

Sun Nov 1 → Sat Nov 14 · 14 nights · base in Lastarria / Bellavista

Two weeks is the right amount of time here — enough to work the city slowly (three museum days, two neighborhoods you learn by name, a market you go back to twice) and still take four day trips out. Late spring means the vineyards are budding, the pre-cordillera is running with snowmelt, and beach weather is starting on the coast. Base in Lastarria for the walk-everywhere first week, then move to Bellavista for the second when the nights get later.

Leg 02 · Madrid

Late autumn, empty museums

Sun Nov 15 → Sat Nov 21 · 6 nights · base in Barrio de las Letras

Madrid in November is the one you dream about: cool days in the mid-teens, no lines at the Prado, and every terrace tucks a heater under an awning. Six nights is enough for the three great museums, two long day trips (Toledo and Segovia are both under 40 min by AVE), and enough tapas nights on Cava Baja to develop a favorite bar. Land Sunday early enough to still catch El Rastro flea market.

Essentials

  • See: Reina Sofía (Guernica)

Leg 03 · Thailand · Bangkok + Chiang Mai

Thanksgiving under the lanterns

Sun Nov 22 → Sat Nov 28 · 6 nights · 2 in Bangkok, 4 in Chiang Mai

The week is planned entirely around one thing: Yi Peng and Loy Krathong fall on Nov 24–25, which is Tuesday–Wednesday, which is Thanksgiving week. Two nights to get bearings in Bangkok, then fly up to Chiang Mai for the lanterns, temples, an elephant sanctuary day, and a Thai Thanksgiving dinner on Nov 26. End of monsoon, start of cool season — the best week of the year to be here.

Essentials

  • Peak: Yi Peng lantern release (Nov 24–25)
  • See: Wat Arun at sunset
  • See: Grand Palace + Wat Pho
  • Eat: Yaowarat street food
  • Do: Elephant Nature Park (ethical)
  • Do: Thai cooking class
  • See: Doi Suthep temple
  • Eat: Khao soi at Khao Soi Khun Yai
22Sun

Land BKK, Wat Arun sunset, Yaowarat arrive

Land mid-morning after the long Madrid–Doha–Bangkok flight. Check in riverside (Sala Rattanakosin or Sala Arun for Wat Arun views). Nap. River taxi to Wat Arun for sunset — climb the central prang if legs allow. Dinner in Yaowarat (Chinatown): T&K Seafood for the grilled prawns, Jek Pui for street curry, Odean for wonton noodles.

Practical, in one place

01
Yi Peng is the whole reason for the week timing.The mass CAD ticketed lantern release (Chiang Mai Dara Academy) sells out 6–9 months out — buy in Feb/Mar 2026. The city-wide festival is free and, in some ways, better.
02
Book restaurants ahead of arrival.Peumayen (Santiago), Sala de Despiece + Corral de la Morería (Madrid), David's Kitchen (Chiang Mai) all take reservations weeks out.
03
Vaccinations & meds.Nothing exotic required for any leg. Standard Hep A/typhoid update before Thailand. Consider a short Cipro course for the road.
04
Cash.Chile: CLP, cards work almost everywhere. Spain: euro, cards fine. Thailand: baht, cards fine in cities but street food and songthaews are cash — small notes.
05
Adapter.Chile is Type L (3-round-pin, unusual); Spain is Type F/C; Thailand is Type A/B/C. A universal adapter with USB-C PD covers all three.
06
SIM/eSIM.Airalo regional eSIMs for each leg. Chile plan works across Argentina if a Mendoza side trip creeps in.
07
Insurance.4-week policy with medical evacuation. Elephants and Andes reservoirs are the two moments to actually have it.
08
What could kill the plan.SCL–MAD is direct on multiple carriers; low cancellation risk. MAD–BKK is a single-connection long-haul — a missed DOH connection wipes a full day. Build a 4h+ layover, not 90 min.
